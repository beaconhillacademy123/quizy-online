## Rule

No third-party music, sound effect, image, font, icon, video, code library, dataset, or other asset should be included in a commercial Quizy release unless its licence permits the intended use or written permission has been obtained.

## Asset register

For every third-party asset, record:

## Audio

Before commercial release, audit every music track and sound effect used by Quizy. Keep the licence, purchase receipt, source URL, creator name, and permitted-use terms in this repository or the owner's private evidence archive.

Do not assume that a track described as “free”, “royalty-free”, “AI-generated”, or available in a content library is automatically cleared for every commercial use.

## AI-generated assets

Record the tool/service used, account/plan, creation date, prompt or production record where practical, and the service's applicable commercial-use terms.

## Replacement rule

If the licence for an asset is unclear, replace it with an asset whose licence is documented before commercial distribution.

This register is intentionally conservative: absence of an entry is not proof that an asset is owned or licensed.
