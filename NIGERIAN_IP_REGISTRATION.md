## Copyright

The Nigerian Copyright Commission states that copyright protection arises automatically when an original work is created; registration is voluntary, but the registration record/certificate can provide useful independent evidence of creation, ownership and related facts.

For Quizy, prepare:

- owner/author details: Uche Promise Egbe / Promise U. Egbe;
- title: Quizy — Adventure Quiz;
- a dated copy of the source/product being registered;
- the relevant original game content and documentation;
- evidence of creation/development history;
- details of any co-authors, assignments or licensed material, if applicable.

Apply through the Nigerian Copyright Commission's NCeRS portal and retain the submitted work, application, payment evidence, certificate and any correspondence.

## Trademark

Copyright registration does not replace trademark protection for the **Quizy** brand.

For the word mark and logo:

1. conduct an availability/conflict search;
2. identify the relevant Nice classes;
3. file through the official Nigerian Intellectual Property Office/Trademark Registry;
4. retain the application number, acknowledgement, examination/acceptance documents, publication record, certificate and renewal dates.

Do not represent the mark as registered until registration has actually been granted.

## Evidence archive

Keep a private archive outside the public repository containing:

- release ZIPs;
- source snapshots;
- Supabase exports;
- screenshots/video;
- Git commit SHAs;
- deployment records;
- SHA-256 hashes;
- original artwork and design files;
- audio/music licences;
- AI-generation records and applicable commercial-use terms;
- domain registration records;
- copyright/trademark applications and certificates.

This checklist is for preparation and record-keeping and is not legal advice.
