## Brand

Primary brand: **Quizy**  
Product: **Quizy — Adventure Quiz**

The product name and logo should be treated separately from the copyright in the game's code and creative content.

## Nigeria trademark action

Before filing, perform an official availability/conflict search for:

1. the word mark **QUIZY**;
2. the Quizy logo/device mark, if protection for the logo is desired;
3. the relevant goods/services classes for educational software, downloadable/mobile software, online educational services, and related activities.

Use Nigeria's official Intellectual Property Office/Trademark Registry for the search and filing process. Do not assume that an application guarantees registration; the Registry examines and publishes accepted applications and allows opposition.

## Domain protection

Reserve and control the important Quizy domain names that are realistically useful for the product, especially the primary commercial domain selected for the brand. Keep domain registrar credentials, renewal dates, DNS records and ownership email under the owner's control.

The current Vercel deployment address is a hosting address, not a substitute for owning a dedicated brand domain.

## Brand evidence

Keep dated copies of:

- the original Quizy logo files;
- brand guidelines;
- screenshots of the product;
- app-store/deployment records;
- domain registration records;
- trademark searches and applications;
- dated marketing materials.

This document is a protection checklist, not legal advice.
